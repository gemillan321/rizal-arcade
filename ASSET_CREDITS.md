# Visual asset credits

Rizal Arcade uses locally hosted historical images whose source pages explicitly mark them as public domain or CC0. They are used for educational presentation and are credited here even where attribution is not legally required.

- **Portrait of José Rizal (1883)** — painting by Félix Resurrección Hidalgo; reproduction released under CC0. [Wikimedia Commons source and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Portrait_of_Jos%C3%A9_Rizal_(1883)_with_frame.jpg)
- **José Rizal signature** — reproduced from Austin Craig’s 1914 book; public domain. [Wikimedia Commons source and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jose_rizal_signature.svg)
- **Noli Me Tangere cover (c. 1887)** — public-domain mechanical scan of a public-domain original. [Wikimedia Commons source and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Noli_Me_Tangere.jpg)
- **Rizal letter to Marcelo H. del Pilar (1889)** — written by José Rizal; public domain. [Wikimedia Commons source and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rizal_letter.png)
- **Manila and suburbs map (1898)** — Francisco J. de Gamoneda; public domain. [Wikimedia Commons source and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Manila_and_suburbs_1898.jpg)
- **Rizal Died for You: Be Worthy of Him** — U.S. government poster; public domain. [Wikimedia Commons source and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rizal_Died_for_You-_Be_Worthy_of_Him_-_NARA_-_5730063.jpg)
- **Rizal Shrine in Calamba** — photograph by Judgefloro, released under CC0. [Wikimedia Commons source and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Northern_fa%C3%A7ade_of_Rizal_Shrine_in_Calamba_with_its_main_door_and_historical_marker.jpg)
- **José Rizal at eighteen** — public-domain photograph of Rizal during his period as a medical student at the University of Santo Tomas. [Wikimedia Commons source and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rizal-18.jpg)
- **Former Central University and Cardenal Cisneros Institute, Madrid** — photograph released into the public domain by its author. [Wikimedia Commons source and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Universidad_Central_e_Instituto_Cardenal_Cisneros.jpg)

The generated `og-arcade.png` social preview was created for this project using the public-domain portrait, map, and book cover above as reference material.

The generated `novel-character-sprite-v2.webp` contains artistic interpretations of six fictional characters from *Noli Me Tangere* and *El Filibusterismo*. These illustrations are not historical photographs or canonical portraits; the game labels them accordingly and uses source-backed text clues for identification.
